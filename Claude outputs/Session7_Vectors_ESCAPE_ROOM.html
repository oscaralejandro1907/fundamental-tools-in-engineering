03Route arithmetic Sealed

Before you compute anything: will u + v point more east or more west? More north or more south? Say it out loud as a team, then check whether the numbers agree.

On paper as well, not entered here: u − v; check that u + (−u) = 0; and settle whether |3u| = 3|u|.

|u+v| = 6.32 km at 108°. Adding the magnitudes instead would have given 10.39 km — the same error the class made in the very first vector session, one week older. And 108° is quadrant II again, so arctan needed the same 180° correction as D. One more thing worth keeping: |3u| = 3|u| = 15 exactly. Scaling stretches a vector; it never turns it.

Twelve dot products by hand is about eight minutes. In a spreadsheet it is =SUMPRODUCT() and thirty seconds. Your clock is running — your call.

Three of the four in Part A were zero, and only the first was obvious. (3,4)·(−4,3) = −12 + 12: that pair is perpendicular and nothing in the components announces it — (−4, 3) is (3, 4) turned through 90°. That is what the dot product is for. It detects perpendicularity you cannot see, and its sign tells you whether motion helps or fights a direction. Seven legs of twelve were driven against the lane, and P9 was exactly across it: full speed, zero progress.
