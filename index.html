The challenge

Design and evaluate
a mini electric vehicle

Across the block your team designs, builds and tests a battery-powered scale vehicle that has to travel a straight track. This module supplies the tools that make the testing mean something: units, precision, measurement, data, interpretation.

Read the brief ▸

What this module contributes

  • Applying basic mathematical tools to a physical problem you can hold in your hands.
  • Using computational tools to manipulate and visualise your own experimental data.
  • Interpreting results — not just producing them — as part of the solution.
Rule of the room

A number without a unit is not an answer. A number with more digits than you measured is a lie with decimals on it.

Where this is going

Six sessions, one vehicle

Session 01 · Live

Units, precision and the cost of getting them wrong

Mission Control. Six rounds of telemetry, two attempts each, one Mars orbiter lost to a unit error.

Session 02

Measuring the vehicle for real

Stopwatch, tape, multimeter. Raw data collection and the first honest look at scatter.

Session 03

The power budget

Electrical behaviour of the drivetrain. What the pack promises versus what the track takes.

Session 04

Uncertainty, and admitting it

How wrong is your measurement, and how does that error travel through a calculation?

Session 05

From data to a model

Fit, plot, predict. Then run the vehicle again and check whether the model earned its keep.

Session 06

Defend the numbers

Final telemetry review. Every figure on your slide is fair game for the rest of the room.
